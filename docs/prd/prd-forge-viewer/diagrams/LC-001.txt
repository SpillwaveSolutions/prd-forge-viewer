LC-001 A section of the PRD set
States: pending, draft, review, final
Starts at pending. Ends at final.

pending --> draft
  When: The author writes the section in the authoring
        tools.
  Rule: The section has content that the authoring tools can
        read.
  By: Author

draft --> review
  When: A grading pass in the authoring tools gives the
        section its grade.
  Rule: A score of 70 or more and 0 blocking defects.
  By: Author

review --> final
  When: The sponsor approves the version.
  Rule: The change log records the approval.
  By: Sponsor

final --> review
  When: The author changes an approved section.
  Rule: The section stays at review until the sponsor
        approves the new version.
  By: Author
